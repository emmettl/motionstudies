Motion Studies

An atlas of movement

Cities move twice

Once through space.
Once through data.

Motion Studies is a catalogue of authored works about transport, geography, and the different ways a moving city becomes legible.

The instrument

Movement is not one kind of knowledge.

01

Scheduled

Timetables become interpolated journeys: the intended choreography of a city.

02

Observed

Broadcast positions become smoothed trajectories: visitors crossing above the plan.

03

Reconstructed

Aggregate flow and speed become particles: measured traffic without imagined tracking.

Not a dashboard. Not a universal map skin. Each work begins with a place, a source, and a visual claim that would stop making sense anywhere else.
