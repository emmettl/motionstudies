# Candidate names and the theses they imply

**Working proposals — 7 September 2026**

[Study index](README.md) · [Catalogue programme](CATALOGUE.md) · [Data readiness](DATA-READINESS.md)

A title should decide what the work is trying to show. It should suggest a sentence about the place, an action in the image and a reason to choose this city. These proposals cover all fourteen candidate cities; the four numbered works keep their identities, and MANIFEST remains the separately named global branch.

The first choice below is an editorial recommendation for discussion, not an assigned edition identity. Alternatives represent different possible works, rather than interchangeable labels. Source feasibility remains as recorded in each dated city brief; this naming pass does not refresh those audits. Local-language meanings are distinguished from our own interpretations. Local readers should test register and associations before adoption; availability and name collisions have not been cleared.

## Amsterdam

**Fietsen — An Amsterdam motion study**

*Countless individual movements. A city taking shape.*

**Selected by the author — 7 September 2026.** Working title; the study remains unnumbered.

**Thesis:** Everyday cycling makes Amsterdam take shape. Countless small movements across bridges, along canals and through crossings accumulate into the city's rhythm, with trams and metros moving inside that field.

*Fietsen* means both “bicycles” and “to cycle”: the title holds the moving things and the act of moving together. Its ordinary language sets the tone for a work that reveals the structure of an everyday activity. The composition should let that activity draw attention to the paths and connections that sustain it.

**Opening commitment:** begin with measured directional pulses at a few bridge crossings, then widen across connected canal-side paths as a count-conserving bicycle field makes the network legible. Preserve pauses and crossing constraints while scheduled trams move through the same composition. Individual particles represent aggregate reconstruction, not observed riders or measured route choices; the opening boundary still depends on adequate counter coverage.

- **Fietsstroom** is a proposed bicycle + flow compound. It would make the collective current the central subject, with less emphasis on the ordinary act of cycling.
- **Fietslicht** is a proposed bicycle + light title. It would make luminous traces the defining visual idea and intentionally echo Gleislicht.

**Direction:** carry Fietsen and its thesis into the source proof and composition work. The line “Countless individual movements” describes the subject of the study; it does not change the aggregate evidence contract. [Amsterdam brief](AMSTERDAM.md).

## Berlin

**Umlauf — A Berlin motion study**

**Thesis:** The Ringbahn continually draws an inside around Berlin, while radial routes and interchanges keep opening that boundary to the surrounding city.

*Umlauf* carries the sense of circulation or revolution. The dictionary meaning supports the motion; interpreting that circulation as an urban boundary is our authored argument. [Duden](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Umlauf).

**Opening commitment:** follow the two opposed circulations, then let a crossing service pull attention through the Ring at a source-backed interchange. The same clock should reveal both enclosure and permeability. A railway boundary must not be presented as proof of a uniform social divide.

- **Innen / Außen** — “inside / outside” — makes the boundary explicit. Stronger conceptually, but it risks turning a porous relationship into a binary.
- **Ringwerk** is proposed here as an authored compound suggesting a work or mechanism of rings, not as an established Berlin transport expression. It emphasises construction and machinery, with less human geography.

**Judgment:** Umlauf is the most durable balance of local language, motion and room for interpretation. [Berlin brief](BERLIN.md).

## Chicago

**Above Grade — A Chicago motion study**

**Thesis:** Chicago's Loop lifts the street grid into a moving architectural structure: trains turn with blocks and travel between façades above everyday street life.

The technical phrase also suggests an altered viewpoint. It tells us where to put the camera. CTA confirms that “L” derives from “elevated” while its system also includes surface and underground routes; the title therefore belongs to the opening's architectural argument, not a claim about every Chicago train. [CTA train guide](https://www.transitchicago.com/howto/riding-the-train/).

**Opening commitment:** move from a plan of the grid into the space between street, track and building. Corners, structure and measured separation should become the subject as a train passes.

- **Streetwork** treats railway and street as one constructed machine. Broader, but liable to suggest roadworks.
- **In the Loop** has immediate geographic and conversational recognition. It is less distinctive and does less to demand a three-dimensional scene.

**Judgment:** Above Grade gives the clearest instruction to both the renderer and the viewer. [Chicago brief](CHICAGO.md).

## Hong Kong

**Surface / Void — A Hong Kong motion study**

**Thesis:** Between mountain and harbour, Hong Kong moves through alternating conditions of exposure and concealment: open water, a narrow tram shore and deep station interiors.

The paired words describe the work's perceptual action. “Void” means the physical space cut through the city, not missing communities or empty underground infrastructure.

**Opening commitment:** begin on the ferry surface and move into a sourced section through station volumes. Keep available ferry motion, reconstructed tram motion and structural rail distinct. The disappearance of a camera into a station must not introduce invented MTR trajectories.

- **Between Levels** puts vertical interchange and the traveller's change of level first. It would need stronger pathway evidence and can make the harbour secondary.
- **Harbour Section** is precise and spatially bounded, but reads more like the name of an architectural drawing.

**Judgment:** Surface / Void is the strongest present thesis. A Cantonese candidate should come from a Cantonese collaborator responding to this idea, rather than a literal translation of these English words. [Hong Kong brief](HONG-KONG.md).

## Istanbul

**İki Yaka — An Istanbul motion study**

**Thesis:** Daily crossings make the Bosphorus's two shores into one transport space, with different relationships to the same water above, on and below its surface.

“Two shores” is the intended English gloss. The ferry operator itself uses *iki yaka* when describing cross-strait transport, grounding the vocabulary in local usage. Making those shores into one daily space is our interpretation. [Şehir Hatları](https://sehirhatlari.istanbul/tr/haberler/deniz-ulasiminin-emekcileri-cimacilar-786).

**Opening commitment:** hold both shores in one composition and let reciprocal ferry journeys establish their relationship. Reveal bridges and the tunnel as separately sourced structural states; animate them only where the chosen historical evidence supports it.

- **Between Shores** concentrates on the crossing itself and is accessible in English, but gives up the local phrasing.
- **The Other Side** makes the viewer's changing point of reference the subject. It is evocative, but can imply one shore is the default centre.

**Judgment:** İki Yaka is the strongest local candidate; retain the dotted capital İ. The currently proposed proof would still need an explicit historical date. [Istanbul brief](ISTANBUL.md).

## Johannesburg

**Long Division — A Johannesburg motion study**

**Thesis:** The spatial divisions inherited from apartheid continue to consume time, money and access in journeys across Johannesburg's city-region.

“Long division” binds separation to its continuing duration and cost. It could be a forceful title, but its mathematical neatness may feel reductive to the people whose journeys the work describes. It is a discussion candidate for local collaborators, not an identity selected on their behalf.

**Composition commitment, pending partnership:** let locally chosen journeys test geographic distance against travel time, transfers and cost. Include minibus transport on evidence comparable to the other modes. This is a proposed question for the partnership; the existing brief's pause on visual development remains in place.

- **Unequal Reach** makes access to opportunities the explicit subject. It is clearer but more analytical, and requires actual accessibility evidence.
- **Ways Through** foregrounds the routes and practices people use to connect the city. It leaves more room for agency, but can soften the structural argument.

**Judgment:** Long Division has the greatest expressive force; Ways Through is the most useful counterproposal for local discussion. [Johannesburg brief](JOHANNESBURG.md).

## Lisbon

**Subida — A Lisbon motion study**

**Thesis:** Lisbon's gradients make traction visible: trams negotiate the hills, funiculars confront them directly, and the river offers a different plane of movement.

*Subida* means ascent or a climb. It is an ordinary Portuguese word used here as a proposed title, not claimed as a uniquely Lisbon expression. [Infopédia](https://www.infopedia.pt/dicionarios/lingua-portuguesa/subida).

**Opening commitment:** tilt from the route plan into measured terrain until the difference between a tram's path and a funicular's direct ascent becomes legible. The river can supply a flat reference while ferry motion remains unsourced.

- **By Degrees** links slope with gradual progress. Elegant in English, though the pun requires explanation and could also suit San Francisco.
- **Against the Slope** gives the work a clear physical problem. It is more descriptive and risks making the city sound like an engineering obstacle alone.

**Judgment:** Subida is concise, physical and locally voiced. Lisbon owns ascent; San Francisco's Grip owns the mechanism of gaining traction. [Lisbon brief](LISBON.md).

## Mexico City

**Más Ciudad — A Mexico City motion study**

**Thesis:** Mexico City's movement exceeds what the formal transport map records; the work should make that excess a question of the map's limits rather than the city's absence.

“More city” is the intended literal reading of this authored Spanish title. It is proposed wording, not a documented local idiom. Its affirmative direction is useful: the subject is a city larger than its representation. A Mexican Spanish reader should test whether it sounds like a planning slogan or implies urban expansion instead.

**Opening commitment:** show the dated formal network with an explicit boundary around what its source knows. A later, locally sourced corridor should expand that account. Do not invent a surrounding particle field to illustrate the title, or present the 2022 formal proof as the completed thesis.

- **Mapa Abierto** — “open map” — proposes an account capable of revision. It can sound like an open-data product rather than an artwork.
- **Beyond the Lines** makes the formal network's limits legible in English. It is less locally rooted and can imply an overly tidy distinction between formal and other movement.

**Judgment:** Más Ciudad gives the investigation a productive direction, but needs local linguistic and conceptual review. [Mexico City brief](MEXICO-CITY.md).

## Mumbai

**Up / Down — A Mumbai motion study**

**Thesis:** Mumbai's suburban corridors carry different directional pressures over the day; the same railway changes its apparent weight as the journeys within it reverse.

These are actual railway direction labels. Western Railway describes Churchgate-to-Virar services as Down and the reverse as Up. They must be preserved according to the relevant railway's usage, not translated into screen north and south. [Western Railway's directional service notice](https://wr.indianrailways.gov.in/cris/view_detail.jsp?dcd=7873&did=17352168458425C9E0B5A49439B6DE73EA9E2227A95A2&id=0%2C4%2C268&lang=0).

**Opening commitment, when evidence exists:** keep both directions visible through a morning and evening comparison, with measured passenger volumes changing their weight. Train frequency alone cannot deliver the title's proposed thesis. The source block remains.

- **Tidal** turns daily reversal into a coastal metaphor. Visually strong, but may suggest actual tides determine the railway's movement.
- **Peak Direction** makes the operational asymmetry exact. Less evocative, and could narrow the work to rush hours alone.

**Judgment:** Up / Down has the richest connection between local transport speech and visual argument. Unlike New York's Local / Express, its distinction is direction and load, not stopping pattern and overtaking. [Mumbai brief](MUMBAI.md).

## San Francisco

**Grip — A San Francisco motion study**

**Thesis:** San Francisco's transport changes its relationship to the ground as it meets hill, tunnel and bay: gripping to climb, passing beneath and releasing onto water.

The mechanical anchor is concrete: cable-car operators engage and release a moving cable using the grip mechanism. Extending “grip” across the city's other modes is our metaphor, not a description of their propulsion. [SFMTA's explanation](https://www.sfmta.com/blog/muni-welcomes-two-new-women-cable-car-grips).

**Opening commitment:** begin close enough to a cable-car climb that the relation between route and relief matters, then widen toward Market Street's layers and the bay. The title earns its broader scope only if this expansion remains one coherent argument.

- **Relief** combines terrain with the experience of finding a way through it. Better if the work becomes broadly about topography; less locally specific.
- **The Slot** centres the narrow street opening above the cable mechanism. It would suit a much closer cable-car study, but under-describes the regional modes.

**Judgment:** Grip is among the strongest names in the slate: short, materially grounded and capable of directing the camera. [San Francisco brief](SAN-FRANCISCO.md).

## Singapore

**Lines Ahead — A Singapore motion study**

**Thesis:** Singapore's railway exists simultaneously as operating service and as a set of commitments about the city's future form; the work makes the distance between those times visible.

The name reads spatially and temporally. It gives planning a visible role without assuming that every announced line will become an operating railway on schedule.

**Opening commitment:** let today's scheduled trains move while the viewer introduces separately dated construction and proposal layers. Future infrastructure remains structural and conditional. The signature action is a change in planning horizon alongside the service-day clock.

- **Future Tense** foregrounds uncertainty and the grammar of promises. Stronger if the work compares successive plans with what was built; that requires archived plans and outcomes.
- **By Design** makes deliberate urban coordination the thesis. It is succinct, but can sound like an uncritical endorsement of planning success.

**Judgment:** Lines Ahead is the best match for the existing brief. Future Tense is the more searching alternative if the historical evidence supports it. [Singapore brief](SINGAPORE.md).

## Sydney

**Water Between — A Sydney motion study**

**Thesis:** Sydney's harbour creates different kinds of nearness: water interrupts some routes, concentrates others into fixed crossings and becomes a direct corridor for ferries.

The unfinished-sounding phrase asks “between what?” The motion can answer with actual connections between shores and neighbourhoods. It gives the harbour a relational role without making it the entire transport network.

**Opening commitment:** hold a pair of shores in view while ferry movement and sourced fixed crossings reveal different paths between them. If the work later compares journey times, include the relevant waiting and transfer evidence instead of comparing vehicle speeds alone.

- **Crossings** is clear and flexible, but too transferable across the water cities to be the first choice.
- **Harbour Time** shifts attention to schedules and connections organised around the water. It is more atmospheric and needs a stronger temporal study to distinguish itself.

**Judgment:** Water Between best separates Sydney's interrupted geography from Venice's water streets and Istanbul's reciprocal shores. [Sydney brief](SYDNEY.md).

## Tokyo

**NORIKAE — A Tokyo motion study**

[Existing study repository](https://github.com/emmettl/norikae)

**Thesis:** Tokyo becomes legible through the changes that connect its overlapping rail systems: line, level, service pattern and operator do not all change at the same place or in the same way.

Retain the existing working title. *Norikae* refers to changing vehicles or transferring; its usefulness is that it names an act, not a visual style. [Japanese dictionary entry](https://dictionary.goo.ne.jp/word/%E4%B9%97%E6%8F%9B%E3%81%88/).

**Opening commitment:** a sourced station complex should reveal the difference between an actual transfer and a train continuing across an operator boundary. A bounded Toei proof can test part of this; the complete multi-operator thesis remains subject to its source gates.

- **Through Lines** would make continuity across operator boundaries the principal subject. It needs durable cross-feed identity evidence and gives less attention to the act of changing trains.
- **Between Systems** would foreground operators, fare areas and institutional seams. More analytical, less immediate and less locally voiced.

**Judgment:** NORIKAE remains stronger than either alternative. Its distinction from Paris's Correspondances should be the specific friction and continuity between overlapping systems, rather than interchange in general. [Tokyo brief](TOKYO.md).

## Venice

**Waterlines — A Venice motion study**

**Thesis:** Repeated vaporetto journeys turn the lagoon into streets and landing stages into the hinges of a city whose transport geometry is made from water.

The title joins water, service lines and the act of drawing. It leaves room for both the Grand Canal's intimacy and the wider lagoon. It must not imply that the work measures tidal waterlines.

**Opening commitment:** begin with little land detail and let scheduled boat journeys make the water network readable. Transform that geography into a lagoon diagram while preserving each trip's progress. The geometry is published service geometry, not an observed wake.

- **Imbarcadero** puts the landing stage and the act of boarding at the centre. Local transport usage is evidenced by Venezia Unica's references to *imbarcaderi*. It would suit a work about the interface between land and water more than the whole moving lagoon. [Venezia Unica](https://tripplanner.veneziaunica.it/it/node/4483).
- **Moving Streets** states the existing thesis directly. It is vivid, though less compact and more overtly metaphorical than Waterlines.

**Judgment:** Waterlines is the strongest network-scale title. Imbarcadero is the more intimate alternative if the work moves toward landing-stage choreography. [Venice brief](VENICE.md).

## How to choose

Read each candidate in its full lockup: title, “A [city] motion study”, then the thesis sentence. The title should make that sentence easier to remember. Next, describe the first thirty seconds without explaining the name: the image should earn it.

**Fietsen** is the author's selected Amsterdam working title. The other clearest names to carry into a next design conversation are **Grip**, **Above Grade**, **Umlauf**, **Subida**, **İki Yaka**, **Up / Down**, **Waterlines** and the existing **NORIKAE**. **Más Ciudad** and **Long Division** need the most testing for register or representational framing. That editorial judgment is separate from which source audits currently permit a prototype.
