# A national motion study

[Study index](README.md) · [Technical feasibility](NATIONAL-DATA-FEASIBILITY.md) · [Series vision](VISION.md)

**13 September 2026 — proposed thesis and composition.** Unnumbered research candidate; England is the recommended first scope, with Great Britain and the United Kingdom considered separately. No title, implementation or publication commitment is implied.

## The proposition

**England's long-distance railways and local bus services make different versions of the same country, connected unevenly across a shared day. The work lets us move between that national pattern, the groups that constitute it, and the evidence of a single journey.**

This is a hypothesis for an artwork to investigate. London-facing railway corridors, connections between northern cities, and local services around smaller towns suggest different spatial and temporal structures. Their relationship should be discovered through a dated body of evidence. The composition must also admit places and intervals that complicate the opening hypothesis.

The national scale matters because it brings different experiences of distance into one frame. A long-distance service can cross a large part of the map while a local community waits for its next departure. Both belong to the same clock. A dense city and a sparsely served place should each be available to sustained attention, even though their contributions to a national total differ.

The first edition would concern **transport services and their operation**. Vehicle counts do not establish passenger numbers, accessibility, demand, prosperity or the experience of a traveller. Those could become related studies with their own evidence.

## The sea, the shoal and the individual fish

The guiding image is the ability to see the whole without surrendering the particular. Three scales become three ways of attending to the same material:

| Scale | Experience | Evidence available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sea** | Movement accumulates into a national field; corridors and local concentrations become perceptible through time. | The measure, interval, geographic scope, contributing modes and coverage behind the field. |
| **Shoal** | A selected group continues moving while its surroundings recede: services sharing a corridor, approaching an interchange, or departing from a chosen place. | The group's explicit membership rule and the journeys that satisfy it. |
| **Fish** | One service acquires duration and geography: its stops, pauses, progress and relation to the rest. | Its source identity, schedule or observations, transformations, gaps and original records where distributable. |

These scales need continuity in both directions. The clock and selected journey survive the descent; returning to the whole keeps its contribution visible. The viewer comes back with a changed understanding of the pattern.

A shoal is not necessarily a geographic region. It can be a relationship across the country. Its membership must be intelligible: “services calling here between 07:00 and 08:00” is defensible; “commuters heading to work” needs evidence of people and purpose that a vehicle feed does not provide.

## Refining the data/art philosophy

**Patterns may emerge from the records; the route back to those records remains open.**

Emergence here describes the relationship between many local events and a larger visible form. It does not establish that the transport system behaves like an organism, or that its order is spontaneous. Timetables, infrastructure and service decisions are already authored. The work reveals their combined consequences alongside the variations of operation.

The artist chooses the frame, duration, projection, light, scale and sequence of attention. Those choices influence what becomes visible. A five-minute accumulation and a whole-day accumulation make different arguments; a logarithmic brightness scale and a linear one do too. The instrument should let a curious viewer discover these choices without requiring everyone to read a methodology before watching.

Three commitments follow:

- **Aggregation retains a path to its constituents.** A brighter corridor can explain which services contributed, how they were counted and which were excluded.
- **Each source keeps its meaning.** A scheduled journey, a received position and an inferred path can coexist with distinguishable treatments. Missing observations remain missing.
- **Attention is reversible.** A viewer may follow one service, inspect its evidence, and return to the collective composition without losing time, place or identity.

“Raw” means the source records received by the study, preserved with their timestamps and release information. Those records are themselves measurements or institutional representations of the world. Opening them deepens the account; it does not remove interpretation.

There is also a limit to the fish metaphor. Some sources begin with counts. A road field can descend to a detector and a measured interval, but those measurements cannot be unfolded into the identities and routes of individual cars. The descent ends at the finest evidence the source actually contains.

## The first experience

Open on a dated morning, with a restrained coastline and enough geography to orient the viewer. Local departures and longer journeys gradually describe the country. The opening image should be recognisable through movement and sufficiently quiet that a sparse service can be noticed.

The defining gesture is **gather, follow, return**:

1. Hold a place or corridor. Gather the services belonging to a stated interval, preserving their position in national space.
2. Extend or contract that interval. See how the group changes when ten minutes becomes an hour. The same measure and scale remain available for comparison.
3. Choose one journey. Descend into its route while the wider group remains faintly perceptible.
4. Open its evidence. Original reports, inferred spans and timing differences appear alongside the journey.
5. Return to the national field. Keep the chosen journey and its contribution illuminated.

One authored passage should connect a long-distance corridor to a regional interchange and then a local bus service. This shows services meeting in place and time; it must not imply that a particular person transferred between them. A second passage should linger somewhere quieter so that the opening does not equate brightness with significance.

The distinction from [Gleislicht](GLEISLICHT.md) is the proposed encounter between different service rhythms across England. The connection to [All Change](LONDON.md) is continuity through a change of representation. Here the transformation is between collective appearance and individual evidence.

All Change can also contribute the London evidence itself. The same dated services can form part of the national field and resolve into London's local detail, with shared acquisition, identities and provenance. Each work retains its own composition: the national study attends to London's relationship with other places; All Change attends to London's physical and mental geography. This makes the series cumulative—work invested in a particular place can deepen the account of the whole. The [London integration plan](NATIONAL-DATA-FEASIBILITY.md#all-change-inventory--local-inspection-13-september-2026) records the existing artifacts and the proposed sharing boundary.

## A day, a recording and a living work

Begin with a bounded recorded interval and matching schedules. Expand to one complete day once the source and visual proofs pass. A date makes the work inspectable, repeatable and recordable. A day can reveal a rhythm, but cannot establish that it is typical: a second comparable weekday and a weekend would test that later claim.

A live version could eventually let the work change with the country. It would require fresh coverage reporting, operational maintenance and an explicit distinction between delayed observations and predictions. Immediacy is a possible quality of the work; traceability is its foundational one.

## Boundaries and omissions

England is a useful first data and editorial boundary, with cross-border service context retained wherever supported. Scotland and Wales should remain visible as connected places when a selected service continues there. A missing source must not look like a service terminating at the border.

Great Britain offers a coherent rail-led alternative. A UK edition also requires Northern Ireland and deliberate treatment of its connections with the Republic of Ireland. These are distinct source and editorial decisions, rather than interchangeable names for a larger map. The [feasibility report](NATIONAL-DATA-FEASIBILITY.md#geographic-scope) records the available paths.

The initial brief gives rail and buses priority. Metro, tram and ferry coverage should be inventoried explicitly; roads, air and shipping can join only when they contribute to the argument and retain their own evidence semantics. A national view must state its included services rather than imply all movement has been captured.

## What would validate or weaken the thesis?

The first proof succeeds if a viewer can recognise a collective rhythm, discover its contributing services, and follow one back to its evidence without a break in the experience. The aggregate must reconcile with the selected records, and local contributions must persist when their individual marks become too small to draw.

The thesis weakens if the picture depends mainly on familiar infrastructure lines, arbitrary smoothing, feed coverage differences or a London-dominated exposure. It also weakens if the close view is merely a decorative vehicle with no meaningful evidence to inspect.

Before assigning a name or number, review three compositions using the same dated material: the national morning, a regional gathering and a sparse local interval. Test whether the proposed difference in rhythms survives a change of aggregation window and remains visible when source coverage is shown.

The next concrete experiment is **one national field, one regional group, one journey and one source record, linked by one clock and a persistent selection**. Its data acquisition and measurement plan is specified in the [technical report](NATIONAL-DATA-FEASIBILITY.md#bounded-proof-and-decision-gates).
